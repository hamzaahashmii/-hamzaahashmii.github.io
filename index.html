Problems I've solved

The part of the work I enjoy most: reading the error, finding the cause, fixing it properly. A few from my labs.

AADSTS50011

Reply URL mismatch

The app gave each configuration its own ACS URL. Copied the exact value from the app instead of the generic guide.

Invalid thumbprint

Atlassian rejected the sign-in

The certificate in Atlassian didn't match Entra's signing certificate. Re-exported the active one from the right app.

AADSTS50057

"Locked" account, no sign-in logs

The user had been disabled in an earlier offboarding. Re-enabled, checked audit logs, ran the rehire checklist.

AADSTS50105

User can't open the app

Signed in fine, but not assigned. Fixed with the right group, not by turning off assignment.

Permanent

Admin role that never expired

A PIM assignment had been made active instead of eligible. Replaced it with a one-hour, just-in-time activation.

Report-only

Policy didn't seem to apply

The test was hitting another app's session. Used What If, then a clean private-window test to prove it.
